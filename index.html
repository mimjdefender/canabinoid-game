How to Play

Controls

  • Use LEFT and RIGHT arrows to move
  • Press SPACE to switch between THC and CBD
  • Press X to shoot molecules upward
  • Press M to toggle sound

Game Rules

  • THC molecules only bind to CB1 receptors
  • CBD molecules only bind to CB2 receptors
  • Correct matches: +10 points
  • Incorrect matches: -5 points

Click anywhere to start the game music!
